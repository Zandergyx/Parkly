# Parkly 🅿

**Parking, made simpler.**

Parkly is an independent project built to help the community find available car parks with less hassle. Designed with a minimalist, Apple-inspired interface, Parkly brings parking availability, interactive maps, search, and saved favourites together in one simple experience.

The goal is to make finding parking easier, faster, and more accessible for everyone.

## My Vision

I built Parkly with a simple idea in mind: finding a car park shouldn't be more difficult than it needs to be.

By bringing parking information into a clean, intuitive interface, Parkly aims to help drivers make better-informed decisions before arriving at their destination.

## Features

- **Interactive map** — explore car parks geographically.
- **Parking availability** — view reported available car lots.
- **Search and filters** — find relevant car parks more easily.
- **Favourites** — save car parks for quick access.
- **Responsive design** — a considered experience across desktop and mobile.
- **Mobile installation** — working toward an installable Progressive Web App experience.
